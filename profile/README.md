## Sensenet

We build the **Wildfire Intelligence Ecosystem** platform: cameras and sensors at remote wildfire sites, streamed live to the people who
watch them, checked by vision models for smoke and fire, and combined with fire-risk, fire-spread and public fire
data on maps.

Our repos are private.
